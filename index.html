FIELD MANUAL

STAY MOVING.

01 / SURVIVE

Clear ten waves to finish an Expedition. Endless runs continue until you fall. Bosses arrive on waves 5 and 10, then every fifth wave in Endless.

02 / BUILD

Choose an upgrade after each wave. Gain XP from fallen enemies, then choose another upgrade when you level up. Earn new weapons during your run.

03 / CONTROL

WASD moves, mouse aims, left click fires, Space dashes, E releases a close-range pulse. Press 1, 2, or 3 to switch weapons. Esc pauses.

04 / RETURN

Shards bank at the end of every run. Spend them in the Forge for permanent upgrades. On touch screens, drag the left pad to move and the right pad to aim and fire.
